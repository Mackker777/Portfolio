Vision Statement

I want to be able to support my family and provide for those who need help. I hope to be a net positive to those around me despite of my failings and personal flaws.

Mission Statement

I will find the means to improve the life's of my family members and those who can be helped while remaining consistent with my morality and beliefs. To achieve this, I am willing to consider whatever personal sacrifices that may be necessary to achieve this goal.
